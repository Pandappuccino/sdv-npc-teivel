# Devil of Desire - Teivel (Custom NPC)
*Countless ages ago, the Gotoro Empire waged a bloody, devastating war against the Ferngill Republic, a one-sided slaughter that led to numerous casualties. In their bid to wipe out the Ferngill Republic, the Gotoro Empire called upon forbidden, ancient magic, tearing open a passage to the realm of demons in hopes of overwhelming their enemies.*

*Their hubris led to their downfall, as a single demon wielding a great scythe emerged and laid waste to the Gotoro Empire, forcing a retreat and allowing the Ferngill Republic time to heal.*

*Now, centuries later, a humble baker has arrived in Pelican Town, looking oddly similar to the demon in the records of the past...*

---

Welcome to my second NPC mod, and one based on one of my oldest OCs ever! Meet **Teivel**, a sweet and kind young baker with a deep, dark secret, and a history with a certain Wizard...
