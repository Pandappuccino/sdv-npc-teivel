# Devil of Desire - Teivel (Custom NPC)
*Countless ages ago, the Gotoro Empire waged a bloody, devastating war against the Ferngill Republic, a one-sided slaughter that led to numerous casualties. In their bid to wipe out the Ferngill Republic, the Gotoro Empire called upon forbidden, ancient magic, tearing open a passage to the realm of demons in hopes of overwhelming their enemies.*

*Their hubris led to their downfall, as a single demon wielding a great scythe emerged and laid waste to the Gotoro Empire, forcing a retreat and allowing the Ferngill Republic time to heal.*

*Now, centuries later, a humble baker has arrived in Pelican Town, looking oddly similar to the demon in the records of the past...*

---

Meet ~~the Devil of Desire~~ a humble young man operating a little bakery in Pelican Town, **Teivel**! He doesn't talk much about his past and seems to have just arrived in town with his son not long after you inherited your Grandpa's old farm, but he seems quite nice, and his desserts are fantastic! Of course, the Wizard seems to be rather distrustful of him...

## Features
- Two(!!) new NPCs, Teivel and his young son Calder to befriend
- Multiple items and recipes to unlock
- A new shop with custom items
- Several events with branching outcomes
- And more!

## How to Install
Download the mod either from Nexus Mods or on the Releases tab here on Github. Make sure you have all of the required dependencies installed, as well. Unzip the file and place the folder "[CP] Devil of Desire - Teivel (Custom NPC)" into your Mods folder, and you're done!

## Dependencies
If the following mods and frameworks aren't installed, this mod won't load!
- Content Patcher
- JSON Assets

## Configuration Options
- **Seasonal Designs:** You can have Teivel's outfits change based on the season and festivals! Defaults to **true**.
- **Default Design:** If "Seasonal Designs" is set to **false**, you can pick which outfit Teivel wears. Defaults to **Summer**.
- **Flirty Dialogue:** If set to **true**, Teivel's dialogue becomes much more flirtatious towards the Farmer, though he can't be romanced. Defaults to **false**.

## Mod Compatibility
This mod has built-in compatibility for the following mods:
- **Stardew Valley Extended:** Includes patches that relocate Teivel's bakery and adds gift tastes for his baked goods to all SVE NPCs.
- **Mobile Phone:** Includes custom dialogue keys to speak to Teivel (but not Calder).

## NPC Interactions
This mod has built-in interactions with the following NPCs:
- **Aelin, the Last Nature Spirit:** Adds two events, two quests, and a recipe.
- **Lucikiel:** Adds two events and a quest.
- **Romanceable Rasmodius Redux Revamped:** Adds **five** events, three quests, and two recipes, and alters Calder's schedule after a certain point.
- **Alecto the Witch:** Adds two events and a quest.

## Custom Gift Tastes
Teivel and Calder both have built-in gift tastes for the following mods:
- **Bees Plus**
- **Alchemistry**

## Meeting Teivel and Calder
Teivel and Calder do not load into the world right away. They will be loaded in on Spring 13 of your first year, and an event will play when the player enters Pelican Town between 8AM and 5PM that day to introduce them. Teivel's bakery, Devilish Delights, will open a few days later.

## Devilish Delights
Teivel's bakery is a fully-functional shop, selling baked goods and ingredients such as Flour to the player. The shop is open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday, from 8AM to 5PM. However, after a certain event, the bakery will be closed until another event reopens it.

## Gift Tastes
Teivel has a rampant sweet tooth and loves desserts. However, he has a strong dislike for oily foods and spicy foods. Calder, meanwhile, likes sweet things and fruits, but dislikes vegetables (as does any child). His favorite treats? His mama's baked goods, of course!
